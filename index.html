Game Rules

  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • If both players choose the same option, the round is declared a tie.
  • Each round winner gets 1 point.
  • No points are awarded for a tie.
  • The first player to reach 15 points wins the game.
  • Scores reset only after the game is fully completed.
  • Play again restarts the entire game.
